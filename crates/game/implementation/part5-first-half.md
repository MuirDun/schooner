# Part 5 — First Half

**Kind:** Content build (Acts 1, 2, 3)
**Status:** Not started
**Depends on:** Part 4 (Mind) complete

---

## Goal

The first 50% of Kinesis plays end-to-end at vertical-slice fidelity. By end of Part 5, a player can launch the real game (not the playground) and play from the cold open through the end of Act 3: five chambers introducing the verbs in order (hands → telekinesis → combinations → destruction → eye reveal), the first cage with the tentacle gift and throwing tutorial and Child eye introduction, and the five flight chambers of Act 3 — where chamber-state persistence kicks in for the first time, where the hidden room (Room 8) sits with its wall art and desiccated bodies, where unintended-solution arcs first earn Researcher reactivity.

This is the first Part that authors *content* against the mature playground tech. Most chamber construction is composition: place real-art assets, set lighting, set color grade, drop in stateful entities (blocks, plates, cracked walls, eye placements).

## The question this Part answers

**Does the pacing work? Are players reading the eye?**

Pacing is the load-bearing creative concern of the first half: severe-hunger / off / variable per the design's tempo line, mechanic-per-room introduction order, the Act 1 → Act 2 → Act 3 transitions. Eye-reading is the other: by end of Act 3, players need to have absorbed that the eye watches them and that the chamber's mood shifts mean something. If they haven't, the eye-state convention isn't communicating clearly enough — fix before Act 4 depends on it.

## In scope

- Cold-open sequence (red noise + Researcher vocalization + dissolve into Act 1 Room 1)
- Act 1: five chambers built with real art assets, lit per the design, with the mechanic-introduction order honored. Eye reveal staged correctly in Room 5.
- Act 1 → Act 2 transition (faint, wake in cage)
- Cage as a *scene type*: glass-walled enclosure, dirt-and-gravel floor, canopy in one corner, distant red lamps and silhouettes visible through glass
- Act 2: cage layout, tentacle gift choreography (the throwing tutorial object), Child eye introduction at the glass, throwing tutorial soft-escalation (re-position + persistent glyph fallback per `design/acts/act2-respite.md`), the canopy-support wall-art decal
- Act 2 → Act 3 transition
- Act 3: five flight chambers built with real assets, lit with Researcher-attitude modulation; Room 7 dual-eye composition; Room 8 the hidden room with its 10–12-decal extensive wall art and three or four desiccated body assets; Room 9 unintended-solution arcs; Room 10 climactic flight chamber
- Chamber-state-persistence-aware authoring: Acts 3 chambers are built knowing displaced objects will persist; the "Researcher has stopped tidying" beat is staged correctly
- First playthrough-level hunger / attitude / death-threshold tuning pass on Acts 1–3
- Act 3 → Act 4 transition
