# Part 6 — Second Half

**Kind:** Content build (Acts 4, 5, endings)
**Status:** Not started
**Depends on:** Part 5 (First Half) complete

---

## Goal

The game is content-complete. By end of Part 6, every route from end-of-Act-3 to every ending is reachable and holds: Act 4's instrument scene with the touch decision plays as the emotional center of the game; Act 5's labyrinth integrates all four verbs as the final exam; the escape branch runs through its service-space stealth choreography and the fork between the gap and the cage; all six endings (A1 with three Researcher-presence variants, A2, A3, B, C) trigger correctly per their routing conditions; all six epilogues hold as looping play spaces with their menu text and the single "You could have left" address.

## The question this Part answers

**Do the endings land?**

The endings are where the four hours of accumulated state come due. A1 must feel like the warmth-trap the design promises; A2 must read as bleak, verb-less waiting; A3 must read as utility; B must read as freedom-as-being-unwanted; C must read as final. The "You could have left" line, used once and only in return-from-fork epilogues, must land. If any of the endings feels random or unearned, the attitude system or the routing wired up in Part 4 needs work — not the epilogue content.

## In scope

- Act 4 cage: layout reflecting accumulated Child attitude (canopy / toys / food / lighting tint variants), Child eye behavior at the glass throughout
- The instrument: sample-based multi-note audio system specific to Act 4 (one sample per opening, telekinetic force triggers); the Act-4 instrument is also the only piece of authored music that re-appears in Epilogue A1 with the Child's eye no longer reacting
- The touch sequence: three player responses (accept / flee / attack) with their attitude consequences and choreography
- Act 5 labyrinth: a single connected layered space integrating all four verbs; chamber-state persistence active throughout; Researcher-attitude-modulated lighting; absence of the Child's eye in any window (the act's emotional signature)
- Act 5 escape branch (parasite-path entries): destructible-wall identification visual language, transition from clinical labyrinth into the dim-red service space, the previous occupant's hideout alcove with its minimal wall art, the food-storage stealth sequence (scripted tentacle choreography, no autonomous perception), the fork between the gap and the player's own cage seen from outside
- Six endings wired to routing: A1 (Pet) with three Researcher-presence variants by Researcher attitude, A2 (Holding), A3 (Sorter), B (Parasite), C (Discard)
- Six epilogues authored as looping play spaces (each its own scene): Epilogue A1 (Pet enclosure with re-used instrument), A2 (holding cell with the "verb is waiting" rule), A3 (sorter with active hunger), B (parasite network), C (waste chute final death)
- Epilogue menu replacement: the "Exit" item becomes *"оставить его тут... / leave him here..."* localized
- The single direct address: *"Вы могли уйти. / You could have left."* — appearing once, only in return-from-fork epilogues, as specified in `design/acts/endings.md`
